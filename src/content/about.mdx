# About me

I'm Mirza Abrar Baig, a Technical Operations and DevOps Support Engineer based in Pune, India. I have 3.8+ years of professional experience supporting enterprise technology, mission-critical applications and cloud infrastructure.

## My story

My professional journey has focused on the operational side of modern technology: understanding how applications behave in production, identifying infrastructure issues, responding to incidents and helping services remain reliable.

Working with Google Cloud Platform, AWS and Azure has given me exposure to cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes, networking, identity management, storage, managed services and cloud operations. I also work with Terraform, Ansible and GitLab CI/CD to support infrastructure automation and deployment workflows.

I believe observability is essential to reliability. Tools such as Datadog, Grafana, Prometheus and Google Cloud Monitoring help me understand system behaviour, investigate issues and support informed operational decisions.

Beyond operations, I have an interest in cloud security, infrastructure standardization and continuous improvement. I enjoy collaborating with engineering, security and product teams to solve problems that cross traditional team boundaries.

I am actively building toward Site Reliability Engineering, DevOps, Cloud Engineering and Cloud Security roles.

> **My engineering philosophy:** Understand the system. Detect problems early. Respond with clarity. Automate what can be repeated. Learn from every incident.
